# Functional testing

*Page to be written.* Planned coverage:

- The "first signal" moment: feed a 1kHz sine at low level into the input, listen at the speaker through a dummy load (8Ω, 50W minimum).
- Square wave testing (see [oscilloscope](../test-equipment/oscilloscope.md#square-wave-testing)) — fast diagnosis of frequency response, ringing, stability.
- Listening tests: known-quality music source, careful evaluation, channel balance.
- Hum floor measurement: speaker output with no signal — how quiet is the amp? (Compare to the 60–80dB-below-full-output target in [heater circuits](../theory/heater-circuits.md#why-the-heater-ac-frequency-matters).)
- Long-warm-up stability check: voltages and bias one hour into operation vs. just after warm-up.
- The first listening session: pick something you know well and listen for what's *missing* as much as what's there.
