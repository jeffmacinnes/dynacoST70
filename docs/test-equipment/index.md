# Test equipment

Reference material for the instruments used during this build. Most of this isn't needed during routine wiring, but becomes essential during [voltage checks](../bring-up/voltage-checks.md) and [bias-up](../bring-up/bias-adjustment.md).

- [Multimeter](multimeter.md) — the IDEAL 61-327 in this build, with notes on what to look for in any DMM for tube amp work.
- [Oscilloscope](oscilloscope.md) — what scopes show that DMMs don't, the Rigol DHO804 under consideration, and capacitor diagnosis.
- [Probes](probes.md) — DMM probes vs. scope probes, CAT ratings, why HV probes matter.
- [Variac](variac.md) — the AC-DC PowerShack SC-5M, slow bring-up procedure, why a variac is not an isolation transformer.
- [High-voltage safety](high-voltage-safety.md) — **required reading** before bring-up. Filter cap discharge, one-hand rule, what kills you.
