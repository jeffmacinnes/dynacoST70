# Push-pull topology

*Page to be written when the build reaches the output stage.* Planned coverage:

- The basic idea: two output tubes amplify opposite halves of the audio waveform.
- Why this cancels even-order harmonics, including 2nd-order distortion.
- Why even-order cancellation is partly *why people prefer push-pull* and partly why some people prefer single-ended (which keeps the 2nd harmonic).
- DC current cancellation in the output transformer primary, and why that means the core doesn't need a gap.
- Class A vs. AB vs. B push-pull: where the EL34 in the ST-70 operates.
- How the [A-470 output transformer](../components/a-470-output-transformer.md)'s center-tapped primary fits in.
- The connection to [phase splitting](phase-splitting.md): you can't have push-pull without two opposite-phase drive signals.
