# Component theory deep dives

*Page to be written.* This is the catch-all for component-level theory that's deeper than what fits on individual [component pages](../components/index.md), but applies broadly across the build.

Planned topics:

- **Capacitor types** — electrolytic vs. film vs. ceramic vs. paper-in-oil. When each is appropriate. Why polarized vs. non-polarized matters.
- **Capacitor ESR** — equivalent series resistance, why old electrolytics fail this way before they fail in capacitance.
- **Resistor types** — carbon composition vs. carbon film vs. metal film vs. wirewound. Why ST-70 originals used carbon composition and why modern builders sometimes substitute.
- **Resistor noise** — Johnson noise (always there) vs. excess noise (carbon comp specifically). Where this matters in the signal path.
- **Tube physics** — what really happens at the cathode. Space charge. Plate dissipation. The pentode "knee."
- **Transformer leakage inductance** — what it is, why it limits high-frequency response, why interleaving fixes it.
- **Dielectric absorption** — the subtle effect that makes some capacitors sound different from others (or doesn't, depending on whom you ask).
